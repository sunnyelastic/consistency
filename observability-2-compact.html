Frequently asked questions

Observability is the ability to understand what is happening inside your systems from the data they produce: logs, metrics, and traces. It lets teams ask new questions about their applications and infrastructure without shipping new code.

Monitoring tells you when something you anticipated goes wrong. Observability lets you investigate problems you did not anticipate, by exploring all of your telemetry together to find the cause.

Agentic observability uses AI agents, such as Elastic nightshift AI SRE, to investigate incidents for you: testing hypotheses across logs, metrics, and traces, surfacing root cause, and suggesting remediation with transparent reasoning.

Faster root cause analysis, fewer incidents reaching customers, and less time spent digging through dashboards, because machine learning and AI agents surface anomalies and evidence automatically.

Elastic keeps all of your telemetry at a lower cost, with best-in-class efficiency for logs, metrics, and traces, and teams can migrate quickly while saving up to 50% of their bill.
